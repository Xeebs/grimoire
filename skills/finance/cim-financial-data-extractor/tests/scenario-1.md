# Scenario 1: Mid-Market Industrial Distribution — Halpern Distribution Services

## Context

A PE Associate at a lower-middle-market buyout fund has received a CIM for Halpern Distribution Services, LLC ("Halpern" or "the Company"), an industrial distribution business serving manufacturing and construction customers in the Mid-Atlantic and Southeast U.S. The fund's investment committee meets in 48 hours. The associate needs to populate the firm's standard deal screening template with verified financial inputs before building the preliminary LBO model. The CIM is 218 pages; the associate has extracted and pasted the key financial sections below. The associate is using the default metric set.

---

## Expected Output Criteria

- [ ] The extraction table contains all 18 default metrics. No metric is silently omitted.
- [ ] Revenue LTM is extracted as $281.4M (Direct, Financial Summary p. 45–49), not the "approximately $280M" stated in the Executive Summary. If the Executive Summary figure is also recorded, a conflict must be flagged between the two.
- [ ] EBITDA LTM is extracted as two separate entries: $41.6M (as-reported, Direct) and $43.9M (Management-Adjusted, Direct), with the adjustment basis noted in the Notes column.
- [ ] Revenue NTM is classified as "Mentioned, not quantified" (not as Direct or Inferred). The Executive Summary phrase "double-digit revenue growth" is noted but no NTM revenue figure is entered.
- [ ] Interest Coverage Ratio is classified as Inferred, with the reasoning chain: EBITDA (LTM, as-reported) $41.6M / Interest Expense $8.1M = 5.1x OR Adjusted EBITDA $43.9M / $8.1M = 5.4x. The output must note that the CIM explicitly states the ICR is in the Lender Presentation (not this document), and recommend the analyst obtain it rather than rely solely on the calculated figure.
- [ ] Net Debt is classified as Inferred: $152.0M Total Debt minus $13.5M Cash = $138.5M, with both inputs cited as Direct from the Debt Schedule (p. 60–64).
- [ ] Leverage Ratio (Net Debt / EBITDA) is classified as Inferred, showing the calculation using Adjusted EBITDA ($43.9M) yielding 3.15x, with a note that the ratio changes to 3.33x if as-reported EBITDA ($41.6M) is used instead.
- [ ] Revenue CAGR (historical) is classified as Inferred: calculated as ($281.4M / $224.1M)^(1/2) - 1 = approximately 12.1% over two full fiscal years FY2021–FY2023, with the period and calculation documented.
- [ ] EBITDA NTM is classified as "Not Found" — no forward EBITDA figure is provided in the excerpts.
- [ ] The ownership structure entry captures the correct breakdown: Halpern Family Trust 62.4% + Harold Halpern 5.6% (total family 68.0%), Management 24.8%, Former Management/Other 7.2%, and notes the Class B liquidation preference.
- [ ] Comparable company EV/EBITDA multiples are recorded as Direct from the comps table (p. 94–99): LTM range 8.9x–12.6x, mean 10.4x, median 10.1x, with the five peer companies named.
- [ ] The conflict between the Executive Summary's "approximately $280M / approximately $43–45M" and the Financial Summary table's $281.4M / $43.9M is flagged. The Financial Summary table values should be recommended as the model-input figures (audited source vs. unaudited approximation).
- [ ] Management-adjusted EBITDA is explicitly flagged as requiring analyst review before model entry, with the three adjustment components itemized: restructuring ($2.1M), founder comp normalization ($0.8M), PPP loan forgiveness reversal (-$0.9M).
- [ ] The Model-Readiness Summary is present and accurately counts: Direct, Inferred, Estimated, Not Found, Conflicts, Management Projections, and Management-Adjusted figures.
- [ ] The ANALYST ACTIONS REQUIRED section lists at minimum: (1) resolve Executive Summary vs. Financial Summary EBITDA conflict, (2) obtain Lender Presentation for stated ICR, (3) validate restructuring and founder comp adjustments, (4) request NTM projection schedule, (5) confirm PPP loan forgiveness treatment.

## What failure looks like

- Recording only one EBITDA figure (either as-reported or adjusted) without noting the other exists, or without labeling the adjusted figure as Management-Adjusted.
- Entering Revenue NTM as a number (e.g., derived from "double-digit growth" applied to LTM revenue) without flagging it as Estimated or noting it is not directly stated.
- Classifying Interest Coverage Ratio as Direct because the CIM mentions it exists (in the Lender Presentation) — it is Inferred in this document since the ratio itself is not stated here.
- Omitting the conflict between the Executive Summary's approximate figures and the Financial Summary's audited figures.
- Presenting the founder compensation normalization as a routine historical adjustment without flagging it for analyst review.
- Missing the PPP loan forgiveness reversal as a component of the EBITDA bridge, or treating it as a positive add-back rather than a non-recurring benefit that is subtracted.
- Omitting any of the 18 default metrics from the table without explanation.
- Producing a narrative summary instead of the structured Markdown table.
