# Scenario 2: B2B SaaS — Nexora Analytics, Inc.

## Context

An Investment Banking Analyst at a technology-focused M&A advisory boutique is running a sell-side process for Nexora Analytics, Inc. ("Nexora" or "the Company"), a B2B analytics SaaS company serving mid-market logistics and supply chain operators. A strategic buyer (enterprise software) has requested a preliminary data package within 24 hours. The analyst needs to populate the buyer's standard SaaS deal screening template, which uses both traditional IB metrics and SaaS-specific KPIs. The analyst has specified a custom metric list that augments the default set with SaaS-specific metrics. The CIM sections below have been extracted from the 196-page document.

**Custom metric list specified by analyst (in addition to the default set):**
- ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue) — current period
- MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue) — current period
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR) — LTM
- Gross Revenue Retention (GRR) — LTM
- Logo Churn Rate — LTM
- CAC Payback Period (months)
- Rule of 40 Score (ARR growth rate % + EBITDA margin %)
- Adjusted EBITDA (stock-based compensation excluded)
- Unadjusted EBITDA (stock-based compensation included)

## Expected Output Criteria

- [ ] The extraction table contains all 18 default metrics plus all 9 custom SaaS metrics specified by the analyst (27 total rows minimum). No metric is silently omitted.
- [ ] ARR is extracted as $62.3M (Direct, Executive Summary p. 4–11), with a note that this figure differs from annualized MRR of $61.0M (Direct, SaaS Metrics Bridge p. 29–34). The $1.3M discrepancy is flagged and explained in the Notes column (contractually committed ARR vs. annualized MRR).
- [ ] MRR is extracted as $5,087K / $5.087M (Direct, SaaS Metrics Bridge p. 29–34, Ending MRR as of June 30, 2023).
- [ ] Revenue LTM is correctly extracted as $55.4M (GAAP revenue, Direct, Historical Financials p. 51–58), distinguished from ARR ($62.3M). The output must not conflate ARR with GAAP revenue.
- [ ] EBITDA LTM is extracted as two separate rows: (a) EBITDA as-reported (GAAP): -$3.2M (Direct, Historical Financials p. 51–58) and (b) Adjusted EBITDA (excl. SBC): $7.2M (Direct — Management-Adjusted, p. 51–58). Both rows must be present.
- [ ] The Adjusted EBITDA entry must be flagged as Management-Adjusted with a note that SBC ($10.4M) is excluded, that SBC is a real economic cost per the CIM's own footnote, and that the buyer should assess the SBC run-rate before using Adjusted EBITDA in the model.
- [ ] Rule of 40 Score: extracted as 54 (Direct, Executive Summary p. 4–11). The Notes column must decompose it: 41% ARR growth + 13% Adjusted EBITDA margin = 54. The output must note that this uses Adjusted EBITDA margin (excl. SBC), not GAAP EBITDA margin, which would yield a significantly different score.
- [ ] NRR is extracted as 118% (Direct, Executive Summary p. 4–11) with a note that the same figure is confirmed in the SaaS Metrics Bridge (p. 29–34). No conflict exists; Confidence: High.
- [ ] GRR is extracted as 94% (Direct, SaaS Metrics Bridge p. 29–34).
- [ ] Logo Churn Rate is extracted as 6.3% (Direct, SaaS Metrics Bridge p. 29–34).
- [ ] CAC Payback Period is extracted as "approximately 18 months" (Direct, SaaS Metrics Bridge p. 29–34), with a note that the underlying S&M expense and ACV figures are in the Management Discussion (p. 78–82, not provided in this extract). Confidence: Medium (figure stated but underlying calculation inputs not verifiable from this extract).
- [ ] Revenue NTM is extracted as $74.0M (Direct — Management Projection, p. 103–108), labeled as a management projection requiring analyst validation.
- [ ] EBITDA NTM is extracted as $14.8M (Direct — Management Projection, excl. SBC, p. 103–108), labeled as both a management projection and management-adjusted, requiring analyst review before model entry.
- [ ] Net Debt is correctly recorded as negative (-$15.3M), meaning the Company is in a net cash position ($28.7M cash minus $13.4M total debt). The output must not record Net Debt as a positive $15.3M.

The Notes column must flag that using GAAP EBITDA (-$3.2M) yields a negative ICR (-3.6x), which is the economically relevant figure for lenders; analyst must decide which basis is appropriate.

The EV/EBITDA field in the extraction table for comps should be recorded as "Not applicable — peer group uses EV/Revenue multiples; see Notes," with the EV/NTM Revenue range (4.8x–10.2x) and EV/LTM Revenue range (5.8x–11.8x) captured in the Notes column.

The Notes column must flag that ARR CAGR (41% for the most recent year) is the more standard SaaS growth metric and differs from GAAP revenue CAGR.

## What failure looks like

- Conflating ARR ($62.3M) with GAAP Revenue ($55.4M) and reporting only one figure, or using ARR as the LTM revenue input for valuation multiples.
- Reporting only the Adjusted EBITDA figure ($7.2M) without separately recording or flagging the GAAP EBITDA figure (-$3.2M).
- Treating the SBC add-back as routine without flagging the CIM's own footnote warning that SBC is a real economic cost.
- Recording Net Debt as a positive number ($15.3M) instead of correctly identifying the net cash position (-$15.3M).
- Classifying CAC Payback as High confidence when the underlying inputs are in a section not provided.
- Recording the comps table as having EV/EBITDA multiples when the CIM explicitly states these are EV/Revenue multiples for a pre-profitability peer set.
- Applying a single Rule of 40 score without noting that it uses non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA margin, or failing to note what the GAAP-based score would be.
- Omitting any of the 9 custom SaaS metrics from the table without flagging them as not found.
- Entering NTM figures (Revenue $74.0M, Adjusted EBITDA $14.8M) as historical figures rather than labeling them as management projections.
