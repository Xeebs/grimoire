# Scenario 1: Mid-Market Manufacturer — Term Loan Renewal with Improving Financials and Key-Person Risk

## Context

A credit analyst at a regional commercial bank is preparing the credit memo for a term loan renewal request from Lakewood Fabrication Group, LLC, a mid-market contract manufacturer of structural steel components for the commercial construction sector. The bank has had a four-year relationship with this borrower; this is the first renewal under a new credit officer who has not previously covered this account.

The existing $6.5 million, 7-year term loan was originated in 2020 at SOFR + 225 bps. The borrower is requesting a renewal and a $1.5M increase to $8.0 million to fund a new CNC press acquisition. The company is wholly owned by the founder, Robert Harkins, age 62. No succession plan has been documented in the file. The analyst has the company's financial spread for FY2022–FY2024 and a term sheet prepared by the relationship manager. No formal industry market overview has been provided.

---

### BORROWER AND LOAN SUMMARY
**Borrower legal name:** Lakewood Fabrication Group, LLC
**State of formation:** Ohio
**Business description:** Contract manufacturer of structural steel components (beams, columns, connection plates, custom weldments) for general contractors and steel erectors serving the commercial construction market in the Midwest. Primary end markets: warehouses, distribution centers, multi-family residential, light industrial. No government/defense contracts.
**Industry / SIC:** SIC 3441 — Fabricated Structural Metal Manufacturing
**Proposed loan:** $8.0 million senior secured term loan (renewal + $1.5M increase)
**Proposed tenor:** 5 years
**Collateral:** First lien on all business assets; first mortgage on commercial real property at 1140 Industrial Pkwy, Lakewood, OH (appraised value $4.2M, 2024 appraisal); personal guaranty of Robert Harkins
**Guarantors:** Robert Harkins (100% owner)
**Rate / pricing:** SOFR + 240 bps (subject to credit committee approval)
**Loan purpose:** $6.5M refinance of existing term loan (mature June 30, 2025); $1.5M new money for acquisition of a Mazak VTC-800 CNC vertical turning center, to be installed Q3 2025
