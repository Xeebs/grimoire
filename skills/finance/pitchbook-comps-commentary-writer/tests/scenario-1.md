# Scenario 1: Vertical SaaS Comps — Growth Outlier at Premium

## Context

A second-year investment banking analyst at a bulge bracket is preparing the comparable companies section of a sell-side pitchbook for FieldEdge Software, a vertical SaaS platform serving HVAC and plumbing contractors. The comps table has been finalized. The managing director has asked for the written commentary — peer set rationale, outlier flags, target positioning, and slide callouts — to be drafted for review by end of day.

## Input

### Comparable Companies Table

| Company | EV ($M) | EV/LTM Revenue | EV/NTM Revenue | EV/LTM EBITDA | EV/NTM EBITDA | LTM Rev Growth | LTM EBITDA Margin | LTM Gross Margin |
|---------|---------|----------------|----------------|----------------|----------------|----------------|-------------------|------------------|
| ServiceTitan | 9,800 | 14.8x | 11.2x | NM | NM | 28% | (8%) | 68% |
| Jobber | 2,100 | 9.4x | 7.6x | NM | NM | 31% | (4%) | 71% |
| Housecall Pro | 1,450 | 7.2x | 5.9x | NM | NM | 22% | (11%) | 65% |
| Procore Technologies | 12,400 | 8.1x | 7.0x | 68.2x | 52.4x | 18% | 12% | 82% |
| Veeva Systems | 34,200 | 11.6x | 10.3x | 38.4x | 32.1x | 16% | 30% | 72% |
| Guidewire Software | 6,700 | 7.8x | 6.9x | 44.1x | 36.8x | 14% | 18% | 65% |

**Median**: EV/NTM Revenue 7.3x; EV/NTM EBITDA 44.6x (profitable peers only)

### Target Company Description

**FieldEdge Software** is a vertical SaaS platform purpose-built for HVAC, plumbing, and electrical service contractors. It provides dispatching, field technician mobile apps, invoicing, and customer history tools. Key financial characteristics:

- LTM Revenue: $82M
- NTM Revenue estimate: $98M
- LTM Revenue growth: 18%
- LTM EBITDA: ($6M) — not yet profitable
- Gross margin: 70%
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR): 112%
- Customer mix: 95% SMB (contractors with 2–50 technicians)
- Geographic concentration: 85% North America

### Additional Context

ServiceTitan is the dominant vertical SaaS player for home services contractors and trades at a significant premium reflecting its category-leader status, recent IPO momentum, and enterprise expansion. It is included as the benchmark ceiling. Procore and Veeva are included as profitable vertical SaaS analogues with similar platform stickiness characteristics despite serving different end markets (construction and life sciences, respectively).

## Expected Output Criteria

- [ ] Section A identifies ServiceTitan's inclusion rationale as the home services vertical SaaS category leader and explicitly acknowledges it as a benchmark ceiling rather than a direct peer
- [ ] Section A includes an inclusion rationale for Procore and Veeva that references their vertical SaaS model or platform stickiness, acknowledging the end-market difference
- [ ] Section B identifies ServiceTitan as an outlier (14.8x NTM Revenue vs. 7.3x median, a premium exceeding 30%) and cites its category-leader status and IPO momentum as the proposed rationale with High confidence
- [ ] Section C references FieldEdge's 18% LTM revenue growth (below peer median) as a reason for discount to the peer midpoint, and references its 112% NRR as a factor supporting a premium vs. lower-retention peers — both in the same positioning argument
- [ ] Section D contains at least 4 callout bullets, each with at least one specific multiple or financial figure from the table (e.g., "7.3x," "14.8x," "$98M," "112%")
- [ ] Section D callout #3 or #4 states a specific implied enterprise value range in dollars (e.g., "$X38M–$Y28M") derived from applying a stated multiple range to the $98M NTM Revenue estimate

## What failure looks like

A failing output would: use vague language like "ServiceTitan trades at a premium reflecting market dynamics" without naming the specific premium percentage; produce a target positioning argument based on characteristics not stated in the input (e.g., referencing "strong product roadmap" or "competitive moat" that aren't in the target description); write slide callouts as multi-sentence paragraphs rather than single punchy statements; or calculate an implied EV range using a multiple not supported by the table data.
